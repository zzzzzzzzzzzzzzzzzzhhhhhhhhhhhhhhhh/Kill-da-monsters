HOW TO PLAY

🎮 MOVE & AIM

W A S D move.

Mouse aims. Left click shoots.

Right click throws a grenade.

💥 COMBAT

R reloads.

G throws a grenade.

M plants a mine where your cursor is.

V swings your equipped sword.

🔫 WEAPONS

1–8 and Q W E T Y U I O switch the first guns.

All other guns and swords are available from the Weapon Shop.

Click a shop card to buy or equip it.

🛡 ARMOR & SURVIVAL

Monsters damage your health when they reach you.

Buy armor with B. Your armor is shown clearly on the HUD.

Defeating monsters earns coins, and purchases are saved in this browser.
